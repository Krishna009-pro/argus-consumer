## Evidence handling

The accepted observations are in `data/ledger.jsonl` with source URL, cached SHA-256, page or locator, snippet, period, unit, reporting basis, reviewer, and status. GCPL's 18 entries (L0131–L0148) were visually checked against official reports and filings. Acquisition cohorts remain separate. Dabur FY2025–26 extraction candidates remain unverified and are not approved ledger observations. Missing, ambiguous, conflicting, and scope-mismatched values remain unresolved; none are replaced with zero or generated by an LLM.

The four GCPL published ratios are verified, traceable references, but M01 excludes them because their formulas differ from ARGUS's. The GCPL report uses net sales divided by average opening and closing balances and reports turnover multiples. ARGUS's current inventory-days and receivable-days calculations use closing balances divided by revenue from operations, multiplied by period days. The evaluator correctly reports these as exclusions.

## Work still required before claiming full validation

1. **M01 comparable references:** assemble official published figures with a formula, company basis, operating scope, period, and unit identical to the corresponding ARGUS calculation. The configured breadth criterion is at least four companies, six comparable periods, and every computed metric. Keep every excluded reference and reason in the evaluation output. Do not add derived or estimated values as if they were independently published.
2. **Volume comparability:** obtain volume-growth inputs only when entity, geography/segment, fiscal period, and reported/underlying growth definition match the revenue series. Until then, leave `price_volume` abstained.
3. **Real red-team evidence:** challenge candidate findings only after a persistent candidate exists. The provenance validator is implemented, but there are no qualifying real candidates or reviewed candidate-specific challenge outcomes.
4. **Blind human baseline:** obtain an independent reviewer, anonymize and shuffle ARGUS and baseline outputs, collect scores using `eval/baselines/rubric.md`, and record sample size and uncertainty. An automated run cannot substitute for this human measurement.
5. **Full release check:** rerun tests after any code changes and confirm regenerated dossier links and evidence IDs in the dashboard. The current dashboard AppTest and health check passed. `python scripts/argus.py run-all` measured M05=6 and M15 median latency=12.8707 seconds across six companies.

## Decision rule

Keep detection thresholds and validation gates unchanged. Preserve abstentions for missing or incomparable evidence. Do not call any result a real discovery unless the existing gate passes and each supporting claim traces to reviewed source evidence.
